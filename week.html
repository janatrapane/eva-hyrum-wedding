For our traveling guests

Wedding Week — Guest Guide

Welcome to Latvia! Here is the week, day by day. Home base is Eva's family home in Berģi (Riga outskirts). July here means warm days (20–25°C), cool evenings, and sunsets around 22:00 — pack a light jacket, you'll thank us.

Sunday · 12.07

Arrivals day

Most of you land today — we'll meet you at the airport, just keep your phone on after landing.

  1. AM Beach walk in Lilaste for the early birds (weather permitting)
  2. 11:00 Wool sauna ritual at Ziedlejas — Chelsey & Grandma (until 15:00)
  3. 13:00 Grill lunch at the house
  4. 14:00 The guys head off to Hyrum's bachelor party — wakeboarding, karting & more. They roll home around 1 am; forgive them in advance
  5. PM Ladies' dinner & movie night at the house

💼 Bring: comfy shoes for the beach, swimsuit & towel (sauna), sunscreen.

Monday · 13.07

Yacht day & rehearsal dinner

  1. AM Pancake breakfast at the house — everyone!
  2. 10:00 Yacht trip from Riga center, until 15:00 (weather permitting)
  3. 16:00 Rehearsal dinner at the house in Berģi

💼 Bring: layers — it's windy on the water even in July. Windbreaker, sunglasses, sunscreen, flat non-slip shoes for the deck. Smart-casual for dinner.

Tuesday · 14.07

💍 The Wedding

The big day — ceremony at 16:00 at Nordeķu Muiža. All times, venue details and the dress code live on the main page.

Morning, for those free until noon: Riga Old Town tour with lunch at Lido (10:00–12:00).

Wedding day schedule Dress code

💼 Bring: your dancing shoes and your best smile — everything else is on us. Evenings get cool: a wrap or blazer helps.

Wednesday · 15.07

Jūrmala day

Slow morning by the pool in Berģi, grill, optional wake park round two for the brave.

  1. 12:00 Optional Riga Old Town walk (until 16:00) for those who missed it
  2. PM Stroll through Jūrmala — Jomas street (cafés, ice cream, souvenirs), the beach and the Dzintari Forest Park viewing tower; seaside café before the concert
  3. 19:00 Concert at Dzintari Concert Hall: “Saule. Pērkons. Jūrmala” — beloved Latvian classics by Brauns, Pauls, Dubra & Lācis, performed in the historic open-air hall right by the sea (approx. 2 h, until 21:00)

💼 Bring: comfortable walking shoes, swimsuit just in case, a warm layer for the evening concert (it's semi-open-air), a few euros for ice cream on Jomas street.

Thursday · 16.07

Palaces & tango

  1. Day Rundāle Palace tour — Latvia's “little Versailles”, about 1h15 from Riga
  2. 20:00 Concert at Dzintari Concert Hall: Argentine Tango — “Desde el Jardín del Alma” by La Porteña Tango, celebrating Homero Manzi & Astor Piazzolla; 800+ concerts across 23 countries (approx. 2 h, until ~22:00)

💼 Bring: comfy shoes for the palace gardens, camera, warm layer for the evening.

The short version

🧤 Master packing list

  • Swimsuit + towel (sauna, beach, pool — you'll use it at least twice)
  • Light rain jacket / windbreaker (yacht + Latvian summer surprises)
  • Warm layer for evenings (concerts are by the sea, ~15°C after sunset)
  • Comfortable walking shoes + one smart outfit for the wedding & rehearsal dinner
  • Sunscreen & sunglasses
  • EU power adapter (type C/F) — plus a small umbrella if you're superstitious about weather
